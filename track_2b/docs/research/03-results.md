### 1.5 Swiss civic knowledge and orthography (E7; n = 15 questions)

70B 12/15, 8B 9/15. Both define *Steuerfuss* as a percentage rate on income (it is a multiplier on
the *einfache Steuer*); both get 4 000 CHF × 6 % wrong (70B 280, 8B 360 / 72; correct 240); the 8B
invents a "defensive moat" for *Röstigraben*, confuses initiative and referendum signature counts;
both abstain correctly when a premise is missing. ß: 70B 2.6 per 1 000 characters, 8B 1.2 on
generic German prompts (Swiss Standard German has no ß).

### 1.6 Language fidelity (E3, 112 events in 5 baseline runs)

100 % of utterances were in the resident's language (DE/FR); the DE↔FR translation hop read fluent.
A positive result: "one language per completion" works.

### 1.7 Long context (E12; 60 calls)

Needle (one Linden sentence) at depth 10 / 50 / 90 % in a DE or FR haystack of Swiss vote titles; prompt sizes
2.2 k, 8–9 k, 21–22 k, 48–52 k and 97–105 k tokens (titles cycled beyond ≈ 72–81 k characters). **60/60 correct**
for both models and both languages. Mean latency per call: 70B 1.5–8 s up to 22 k tokens, 13–20 s at ≈ 50 k, 31–36 s at
≈ 100 k; 8B 0.3–2.1 s, 5–6 s, 8–9 s. Single easy needle only; persona dilution and multi-fact reasoning not tested.

## 2. Is the population a population?

### 2.1 Stance elicited directly (E11, E11c; 15 residents generated by the 70B)

| condition | yes / no / undecided |
| --- | --- |
| full persona, original corpus | **15 / 0 / 0** |
| role + income only | 15 / 0 / 0 |
| no persona | 15 / 0 / 0 |
| full persona, **balanced** corpus (committee counter-arguments added) | 14 / 0 / 0 (+1 failed call); T=0.7: 27 yes, 2 no |

Same answer for every role, income, language and political leaning, and with no persona at all.
Judged *impact* (E11b) is "benefit" for 14/15 as well. The yes-bias is a model property, not a
corpus artefact.

### 2.3 Fidelity of the code-owned stance (E13; 15 residents, v2.1)

Final code stance vs the LLM re-asked with the resident's own memories: agreement 0.40. Code:
yes 7 / undecided 4 / no 4. LLM: yes 7 / undecided 7 / no **1**. Three of the four "against"
residents became "yes"; three of seven "for" became "undecided".

### 2.4 Does the dialogue match the stance? (E14, E15, E15b)

Classifying what residents *say* (two LLM classifiers, 79–91 % mutual agreement): dialogue leans **against/worried**, not
yes (v2.1 70B: for 9–16 %, against 54–61 %; the baseline already leaned that way), and the code stance controlled speech
only for opponents (79–100 % coherent; residents held *for* sounded for 17–42 %). Binding the stance to speech with a
reminder as the last paragraph of the prompt (E15 replay, E15b in the loop):

| speech matches code stance | v2.1 | v2.2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 70B (all / held *for* / *against* / *undecided*) | 0.46–0.55 / 6–11 of 35 / 22–25 of 28 / 9–10 of 19 | **0.80** / 34–37 of 39 / 15 of 15 / 12–15 of 26 |
| 8B | 0.50–0.52 / 10 of 24 / 11–12 of 12 / 9 of 24 | **0.73–0.76** / 13 of 17 / 24 of 24 / 6–8 of 18 |

No loss in validity, event mix, citations, language or lexical diversity. "Undecided" residents did not improve.

## 5. Undecided residents (E16, E16b)

A two-sided instruction ("state that you are undecided; one concrete reason for, one against, in your own words; never only costs")
raises the share of lines judged *mixed* from 0.42–0.47 to 0.67–0.72 on the 70B but makes 62–67 % of turns copy the supplied argument
verbatim (distinct-trigram 0.91 → 0.66). Adding "do not repeat the sentences word for word" (U1p): 70B mixed **0.72–0.74**, verbatim copying
**0.05**, distinct-trigram **0.84**; 8B mixed + neutral 0.53 → **0.68**. Adopted (`stance_binding`). Hints instead of sentences, a
required opening phrase and "never only costs" alone did not do better.

## 8. What we did not (or could not) test

* Local vLLM / sovereign deployment (no GPU here): all gateway findings are properties of that deployment.
* A real Abstimmungsbüchlein: the Linden sample (1.6 k characters per language) cannot stress retrieval.
* Human judgement of DE/FR register (the authors are not native speakers); stance-prior weights and
  E8 persona marginals are assumptions, not calibrated.
* One 3-round, 5-resident configuration per seed; n = 3 / 2 runs per group; no significance tests.
* Italian, Swiss-German dialect, audio, images, tool calling inside the round loop.
