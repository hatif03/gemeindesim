Millfield steel and aluminum tariff — sample policy (English)

The federal government will impose a 25% tariff on imported steel and a 10% tariff on imported aluminum, effective in 90 days.

Intended effects: protect domestic mills, restore overtime at the Millfield Steel Foundry, and fund a $40 million revitalization package for the county.

Likely side effects: construction firms report steel beam prices already up 12% on quotes. Auto-parts suppliers employing 15–80 people may delay contracts. Household appliance prices are projected to rise 3–6% in the first year.

Local stakes: mill workers want recalled shifts; fabricators fear input costs; shopkeepers depend on mill paychecks; retirees on fixed incomes watch grocery and hardware prices.

This is a synthetic scenario for GemeindeSim (English tariff sample), not a real statute.
